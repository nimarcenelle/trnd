04 — Compounding

The flywheel is the actual company

Generic signal is table stakes and gets commoditized fast. What compounds is knowing which offer, angle, and audience actually converts — for this vertical, in this geography.

Every clinic that becomes a client connects their ad account as part of onboarding. That real result — did it book, what did it cost — feeds straight back into the next recommendation. It's a dataset a competitor can't buy or scrape; it only exists because a business chose to work with us.

05 — Defensibility

Where the moat actually is

Bar length = how hard this layer is for a competitor to copy — not size, not effort. A short bar means "buildable in weeks." A full bar means "only exists after real campaigns have run."

What "performance data" actually is: results from our own clients — not scraped, not public. Every business that works with us connects their ad account (Meta/TikTok) as part of onboarding, so we can see what actually converted: CTR, bookings, CAC, revenue. That connected-account relationship is the product, not a side effect of it — a competitor without our clients has no way to get this data at all.
